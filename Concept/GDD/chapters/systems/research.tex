\section{Research System}
\label{subsec:research-system}

\subsection{Overview}

The Research System provides long-term, account-wide progression by converting play (XP, kills, completed Maps) into unlocks. 
It controls when new systems, heroes, item tiers and world variants become available and thus paces both power growth and complexity.

Research is organised into multiple \emph{research trees}. For the MVP, three main trees are planned:
\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Crafting Tree} -- unlocks and deepens all item- and crafting-related systems.
	\item \textbf{World Tree} -- unlocks Map modifiers and enemy-ecosystem variants.
	\item \textbf{Heroes Tree} -- unlocks new Heroes, skill tiers and the endgame Crown system.
\end{itemize}

Inputs to the Research System:
\begin{itemize}
	\item XP (global or research-specific, derived from playtime and Map completions),
	\item Kill counts (monster-type / tag sensitive),
	\item Completed Maps and Difficulties.
\end{itemize}

Outputs from the Research System:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Unlocks of systems and content (Hideout elements, Heroes, item tiers, crafting options, Map tiers),
	\item Access to higher difficulties and new enemy variants,
	\item Endgame enhancement layers (e.g. Crown sockets).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Research Trees (MVP Scope)}

For MVP, the Research System is structured into three primary trees: \emph{Crafting}, \emph{World} and \emph{Heroes}. 
Each tree focuses on a specific axis of progression and unlocks both systems and content along that axis.

\paragraph{Crafting Tree}

The Crafting Tree defines how quickly and in which order the player gains access to more advanced item manipulation. 
It starts with basic gear usage and gradually unlocks more powerful and more deterministic crafting tools.

	Unlocks re-infusing items, enabling early deterministic rerolls instead of pure item hunting. \\
	\hline

	\emph{„Find some of the rarer materials, so we can improve the gear.“} &
	Unlocks access to higher gear tiers and rarer crafting materials; raises the ceiling for item power. \\
	\hline
	Refine / Tempering &
	Introduces finer control over item stats via tempering. &
	Unlocks refining/tempering operations that adjust existing stats within defined ranges. \\

	Unlocks stronger infusions and/or more slots per item, increasing build complexity. \\
	\hline

	Gives access to high-end gear bases and top-tier materials for endgame items. \\
	\hline

	Unlocks the highest tier of infusions with strong, build-defining properties. \\
	\hline

	Unlocks over-tempering mechanics that allow pushing items beyond normal limits at significant cost or risk. \\

\paragraph{World Tree}

The World Tree focuses on Maps and enemy ecosystem variants. It controls how dynamic, dangerous and diverse the world becomes.

	\textbf{Node} & \textbf{Short Description} & \textbf{Unlocks / Effects} \\
	\hline
	Empower Maps &
	Enables basic Map empowerment. &
	Unlocks modifiers that increase Map difficulty in exchange for better rewards (e.g. more enemies, higher damage/HP, better loot). \\
	\hline
	Map Live-Events &
	Introduces dynamic events during Maps. &
	Unlocks live events during a Map (e.g. reinforcements, shrines, hazards) that alter Wave flow and risk/reward mid-run. \\
	\hline
	Undead Monsters &
	Expands the enemy ecosystem with undead. &
	Unlocks undead-themed enemy packs, bosses and tags; adds new resistances/weaknesses and synergies. \\
	\hline
	Holy Monsters &
	Introduces holy/fanatic enemy types. &
	Unlocks holy-themed enemies and factions with their own mechanics and counters, further diversifying encounters. \\

\paragraph{Heroes Tree}

The Heroes Tree unlocks new Heroes and deepens their progression layers. 
It is tightly coupled to the Skill System and the Crown System and must be paced carefully to avoid overwhelming players too early.

%\begin{table}[h]
%	\centering
\begin{tabular}{p{3.2cm}p{4cm}p{8cm}}
	\textbf{Node} & \textbf{Short Description} & \textbf{Unlocks / Effects} \\
	\hline
	Hero Packs (Story / Theme) &
	Unlocks groups of Heroes along narrative or thematic lines. &
	Frees 2--3 Heroes at a time, grouped by story or theme, instead of single isolated Heroes. Encourages experimenting with new team archetypes. \\
	\hline
	Skill Tiers &
	Unlocks deeper layers of the skill trees. &
	Opens higher skill tiers (e.g. Tier 2/3 nodes) for existing Heroes, enabling more specialised builds and advanced synergies. \\
	\hline
	Crown Sockets &
	Prepares Heroes for the endgame Crown system. &
	Unlocks Crown sockets on Heroes or their skill trees, allowing equipping powerful endgame Crown boosts once available. \\
\end{tabular}

\subsection{Research Progression and Pacing (MVP-Level)}

For MVP, the Research System should:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Introduce \textbf{Crafting basics} and \textbf{basic Map empowerment} early, to make loot and Maps meaningful quickly.
	\item Gate \textbf{World variants} (undead/holy) and \textbf{endgame crafting (Gear III, Infuse III, Over-Tempering)} behind mid- to late-game play.
	\item Unlock \textbf{Hero Packs} and \textbf{Skill tiers} step by step, so that players first get to know their builds, before they Crown-Sockets come in to the game.
\end{itemize}
